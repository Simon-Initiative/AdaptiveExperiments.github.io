## Experiments-as-a-Service (EASI)

Adaptive Experiments builds Experiments‑As‑a‑Service Infrastructure (EASI) to make real‑world educational field experiments simple, reproducible, and effective. Lower the barrier for teachers and researchers to test instructional designs, discover what improves learning, and deploy improvements at scale.

### What is EASI?

EASI is a platform that enables educators and researchers to design, run, and analyze educational experiments. It provides a suite of tools for creating and managing experiments, including randomization, data collection, and analysis. EASI is designed to be user-friendly and accessible, with a focus on making experiments easy to set up and run.
